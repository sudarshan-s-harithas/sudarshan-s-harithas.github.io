Motion across embodiments.

MotionGlot shares a vocabulary across language, human motion and quadruped trajectories. This demo runs the released GPT-2 and VQ-VAEs locally in your browser. Prompts and uploaded motions stay on your device.

What you’re seeing

Human playback recovers a 22-joint skeleton. Robot playback shows a quadruped marker following generated x, y and heading increments. Leg poses are illustrative and fixed; there is no physics or locomotion controller. Browser weights and computation are documented in the code.
